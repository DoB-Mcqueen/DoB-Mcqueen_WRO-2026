WHAT IS WORLD ROBOT OLYMPIAD?
World Robot Olympiad™ (WRO®) is a global robotics competition focused on creativity, engineering, and problem-solving skills.

WRO is platform-agnostic.
This means schools and teams can choose the robots and technology that fit them best.

Why choose WRO?
Many schools want to invest in robotics kits that can be used over several seasons.

WRO offers:

Freedom to choose your hardware
The ability to reuse what you already own
A competition structure that works across countries and suppliers
WRO compared to other robotics competitions
Some robotics competitions are built around specific hardware or robotics kits.

WRO is designed differently.

At WRO:

There is no required robotics kit
Teams can reuse existing robots across seasons
Schools can source hardware locally, based on availability and budget
Rules define requirements, not brands
Teams are evaluated on how they solve the task, not which robot they use
ALL ROBOTS ARE WELCOME

What this means in practice:

You can use robots from different brands
You can mix kits, sensors, and custom parts
You can use 3D printed elements or locally available materials
Any robot is welcome as long as it follows the rules of the chosen category
(size, weight, sensors, battery power).
