# Mechanical System Problems

#### Backlash in Ackermann Linkages: Loose steering joints or mechanical play causing vehicle drifting and steering inconsistency.

#### Torsional Stress on Differential Gears: Structural wear or high-speed gear slippage on the 3D-printed differential casing during sharp cornering acceleration.

#### Chassis Dynamic Flexing: Excessive vibration or twisting of structural plates affecting sensor stability and wheel alignment.

#### Camera Tower Jitter & Motion Blur: Frame resonance from motor frequencies distorting the video stream and breaking image processing algorithms.
