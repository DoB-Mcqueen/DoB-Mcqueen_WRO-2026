# Electrical System Problems

#### Inductive Feedback & Ground Loops: High-current spikes from the drive motors leaking into the shared ground rail and resetting the microcontroller or single-board computer.

#### Voltage Sag Under Peak Actuation Current: Drop in voltage when steering servos and drive motors draw power simultaneously, causing transient sensor data failures.

#### UART Serial Data Corruption: Cross-talk or electromagnetic interference (EMI) on unshielded signal wires causing packet drops between the Raspberry Pi 5 and the Pico.

#### Thermal Throttling on Buck Converters: Extreme heat buildup in voltage step-down regulators during continuous multi-lap competition runs.
